What the handoff delivers

Design references

Twelve separate section and product-surface plates, exact storyline, proposed tokens, responsive rules, interactions, and implementation requirements. Selection does not imply human approval.

Evidence dependency

No genuine website before/after pair has been supplied. The tablet webpage is now an explicitly requested narrative concept. The genuine evidence slot remains handoff-only; it is not a result claim.

3D source dependency

These plates are still images. Interactive geometry, layers, camera paths, morph targets, and video have separate readiness states in the manifest. A 3D-looking render is not a delivered GLB.

Narrative and outputs remain distinct

Brain, cyborg, fragmented-signal, and refinement scenes are conceptual artwork. Resource previews must show a genuine result tied to the resource, or omit media and use real metadata. Classification appears here and in the manifest, not as decorative product labels.

Motion is specified, not implemented

The future first-visit entrance plays automatically for approximately 36 seconds, with no visible playback or scene-navigation controls. An eligible key or bare-scene tap skips directly to discovery. Returning visits, direct links, reduced motion and save-data bypass it. N6 retains Explore resources and Suggest a resource.
