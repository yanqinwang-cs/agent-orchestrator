# Agent Lab reference board

## Animation coverage: keyframes are not exhaustive

The images in this handoff are selected **key visual states**, not an exhaustive storyboard, complete frame sequence, or finished animation asset set. N1–N6 identify narrative beats, not a six-frame animation. **Additional keyframes, intermediate poses, transition states, camera-reveal frames and rendered/interpolated in-between frames are required** to fill out the animation smoothly. Additional images, textures, masks, layers or scene assets may also be needed; the manifest records what is currently supplied, not a ceiling on production deliverables.

Do not implement the cinematic target as six disconnected stills or assume that a crossfade completes a reveal. Expand and review a storyboard/animatic for every transition before final animation. Preserve approved copy, fixed text anchor, object identity, screen texture, light and projection origin while adding frames. For a 36-second video, 30fps would produce 1,080 rendered frames (24fps: 864); these are delivery examples, not a requirement to generate that many independent AI images. A real-time scene instead needs continuous authored camera/object animation at the device's rendering cadence. Inspect the actual intermediate motion, not just the six endpoints.

## How to read the evidence

- **Seen:** inspected pixels or a directly observed interaction in this session. A still establishes composition and color, not timing.
- **Documented:** the original creator or product source describes a technique or behavior. This is not a local performance or accessibility test.
- **Proposed adaptation:** our design judgment for Agent Lab. It is not a claim about the reference and is not yet user approval.
- **Unverified:** an unavailable property or playback state. It remains explicit rather than being filled from expectation.

The original search-before-generation ledger and stock licensing decisions remain in [REFERENCE_RESEARCH.md](design-handoff/REFERENCE_RESEARCH.md). This revised board adds motion, creator videos, 3D case studies, and the supplied Pinterest references before the new narrative assets are finalized. The archived v1 composition plates remain historical design work.

## Translation into one Agent Lab world

This is a proposed visual grammar for review, grounded in the user's corrected direction. Concrete palette tokens and layout dimensions belong in `DESIGN_SPEC.md`.

| Constant | What stays consistent | What may progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Space | Deep-neutral environment, shared horizon/perspective, a single readable text region per scene | Camera distance and subject scale change gradually. |
| Material | The same graphite/silver substrate and luminous signal material | Roughness and signal organization become more controlled. |
| Light | One consistent key-light direction; bright text is independent of scene illumination | Warm pressure accents give way to cool communication accents, then lower-chroma balanced light. |
| Motif | The same fragments, strands, or information carriers remain recognizable | Tangled, interrupted, selected, aligned, and refined states express the story. |
| Type | One family and stable hierarchy; N1-N5 contain only the Agent Lab name and approved storyline | Position can respond to object placement without inventing an entirely new composition family each time. N6 additionally exposes Explore resources and Suggest a resource. |
| Transition | Carry the same shape or motion direction across the cut | Use controlled match transitions and material continuity; do not morph real evidence pixels. |
| Interaction | No visible Enter, Skip, Previous, Next, Play story, pause controls, or hint captions | An eligible first visit automatically plays approximately 36 seconds, then holds static N6. Ordinary eligible keypresses or a tap/click on bare artwork bypass directly to discovery; native controls retain their behavior. Reduced-motion preferences and direct discovery/resource links bypass the cinematic entrance. |

The emotional arc is overload and confusion, communication friction, selection and translation, refinement, polished output, then calm discovery. The real output portion still needs an actual captured result. A concept scene can prepare that transition but cannot impersonate a resource's output.

The N5 approved storyline question is **“Ever wondered how to build the website you saw on Pinterest?”** Its visual slot must not claim that an arbitrary reference site was built by Agent Lab. Keep input/output provenance and capture requirements in handoff metadata. Do not overlay “Illustrative sample,” “Demo preview,” technical evidence labels, or decorative captions on the public composition merely to explain the handoff to its reviewer.
