## Photography-led design — authored, not validated

Builder-only instructions: use the palette, photographic integration, identity and action guidance below to produce the website, not as end-user copy. Do not output this guidance or its design rationale as customer-facing explanations, captions, badges or website sections. Displaying it in this readable instruction pack is distinct from displaying it on the generated website.

Apply this section when photography leads the composition, not merely because a page contains an image.

Inspect the selected photograph before choosing the page palette. Derive supporting colours from its colours, light, reflections and materials; preserve natural colour and luminance variation. Respect supplied brand and semantic colours while maintaining readable contrast.

Let atmospheric photography flow through the page background, with image-specific tonal transitions and only necessary overlays that preserve the subject and highlights. Fit transitions to that image instead of reusing a stock gradient. Distinguish bounded offers and controls from the scene. Keep detail, gallery, listing and documentary cards bounded when inspection, comparison or reading benefits.

Give a new brand an original, deliberate mark or wordmark that remains recognisable at its displayed size. Make primary buttons obvious through shape, contrast, visual weight and placement, with labels that explain their action.

On narrow screens, recompose the crop and content for a readable sequence and an identifiable subject, without forcing text over photography.
