## Use / avoid

Use: Campaign or product introduction with a shopping or contact entry.

Avoid: Full catalog, cart, checkout or fulfillment system.

Purpose-first category: Landing/campaign; commerce entry. This is framework-neutral English guidance for personal use. Mobile compositions inform app-like frontends, not native implementation.

## Observed composition and page notes

R02: modular watch campaign and aurora imagery. R03: roasting scene, product card, price and add control. R10: large type and chair/interior columns. R11: dramatic shoe image, price and shopping entry.

## Authored priorities

1. Give the product one dominant visual and a clear value proposition.
2. Connect supporting specifications or craft details to a visible shopping/contact action.
3. Keep secondary imagery and promotional details consistent with one chosen campaign identity.

## Allowed variation

Choose restrained editorial R02, photographic craft R03, warm interior R10 or energetic sports R11. Typography, symmetry and palette may change while retaining hierarchy.

Exact fonts, alignment, palette, assets and section order remain open unless essential to the chosen direction. Favor coherent composition and task clarity over source resemblance.

## Authored usability guidance

Keep the action readable over imagery; give meaningful product images descriptive alternatives and decorative images empty alternatives.

Consult [shared notes](../SHARED-NOTES.md) for shared identities and adaptation guidance; these recommendations are not behavior observed in screenshots.
